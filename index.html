Rumble pulses on E-STOP, redline, and low-battery. Road Feel adds continuous engine vibration, rumble-strip buzz on hard cornering, and a launch shudder. 0% disables all. Requires a wheel/pad with a haptic actuator (Chrome).

ESC Calibration Helper

Most ESCs learn their throttle range once. Prop off / wheels up first. Typical procedure: 1) power ESC OFF · 2) hold Full Forward · 3) power ESC ON, wait for tones · 4) tap Neutral · 5) tap Full Reverse · 6) back to Neutral. Follow your ESC's manual for the exact beeps. These buttons send raw DRIVE frames to the selected car (bypassing arming).
